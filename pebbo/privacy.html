Pebbo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Pebbo is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that floats your pictures on your screen. This policy explains what it does with your information.

Data collection

Pebbo collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. Pebbo does not send your data anywhere.

Where your data lives

Everything Pebbo stores stays on your computer:

  • Your settings, your star count and your task progress.
  • The pictures you add yourself.

Pebbo Plus purchase

The Mac App Store version offers an optional one-time purchase, Pebbo Plus, which unlocks all stickers at once. Stickers can also be unlocked with stars for free. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple. Pebbo receives no payment details, and it does not see your Apple account or card information. Pebbo only records locally that Pebbo Plus is unlocked.
